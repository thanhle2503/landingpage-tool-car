About Tool.Cars

Tool.Cars is an independent site that compares car tech you can buy and install yourself: OBD2 scan tools, CarPlay screens and adapters, dash cams, tire inflators and jump starters. It is not the official website of any brand it lists.

How we compare

  • One tab per product type. Each tab starts with a brand-vs-brand table, then lets you put any two products side by side.
  • Prices and specs come from each brand's official store or its Amazon listing, checked on the date shown at the bottom of each page.
  • Customer reviews are real buyer reviews copied from each brand's official store. We do not write or edit them.
  • Our notes say which product fits which driver, based on published specs, prices and buyer reviews. We do not run lab tests.

How the site makes money

Product buttons are affiliate links. If you buy through them, Tool.Cars may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate, Tool.Cars earns from qualifying purchases. Prices are always set by the store, so always confirm the price on the product page before you buy.

What you will find here

  • Scan tools: iCARZONE vs Innova OBD2 scanners, battery testers and borescopes.
  • CarPlay: Youducar vehicle-specific screens vs Carlinkit wireless adapters and AI boxes.
  • Dash cams: ROVE vs Fanttik front, front + rear and 3-channel cameras.
  • Tire inflators: Fanttik cordless inflators, jump starters and car vacuums.
