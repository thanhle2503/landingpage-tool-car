                                            Questions, answered

                                            Short answers based on what each brand lists on its product pages.

                                            What does a cordless tire inflator do?

                                            It pumps up a tire from a battery, without a cord or a gas station compressor. You set the pressure you want and the pump stops when the tire gets there. Fanttik inflators on this page start around $30.

                                            Tire inflator or jump starter: which do I need?

                                            A tire inflator fixes low tire pressure; a jump starter starts a car with a dead battery. Fanttik sells bundles with both, such as an X-series inflator with the Potent T10 1800 Amp jump starter.

                                            Which Fanttik inflator is for pickup trucks?

                                            The X9 Ultra is listed as a tire inflator for pickup trucks, for larger tires that take longer to fill.

                                            Is there a Fanttik inflator for EVs?

                                            Yes. The X8 APEX EV is the EV version of the X8 APEX, also sold in bundles with a Fanttik car vacuum.
