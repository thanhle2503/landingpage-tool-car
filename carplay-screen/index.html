Before you order

Quick answers based on what Youducar states on its store.

Will it fit my car?

Each Youducar unit is built for a specific model and year range, so match your exact model year first. For imported cars, check left-hand drive (LHD) or right-hand drive (RHD) in the product title. If you are unsure about fit, contact Youducar from the product page before you order.

Do I need to cut any wires?

No, in most cases. Youducar describes its vehicle-specific units as plug-and-play: they use factory-style connectors that plug straight into the car's existing harness, with no splicing. Installation usually means removing dash trim panels, which the included manuals and video guides walk you through.

Will my backup camera and steering wheel buttons still work?

Usually yes. Youducar lists factory backup camera compatibility and steering wheel button support for its vehicle-specific units, so the controls you use today keep working. Some vehicles need an extra adapter or protocol box, which Youducar sells separately, so check the product page for your model.

What do the memory options mean?

Most units come in two or three versions, for example 4G+64G, 8G+128G or 8G+256G, meaning RAM plus storage. More RAM gives smoother multitasking with CarPlay, maps and music running together, and more storage holds more apps and offline maps. The price shown on this page is the starting price.

Can I get help installing it?

Yes. Youducar provides installation manuals and video guides for its units, plus direct support from its engineers if you get stuck. A plug-and-play unit is a common DIY job with basic trim tools, and any car audio shop can also fit one for you.

Does it support wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto?

Most Youducar head units list wireless Apple CarPlay and wireless Android Auto, and the product cards on this page show which features each listing states. Because the units run Android, you can also install apps such as Google Maps, Waze or Spotify directly on the screen without a phone.

How much does a CarPlay screen upgrade cost?

Youducar screens on this page start at about $210 and most popular vehicle-specific units for Toyota, Ford, Chevy and Jeep cost $500 to $1,100 depending on screen size and memory. Use the code YDTHANH10 for 10% off orders over $300, entered at checkout on youducar.com.
